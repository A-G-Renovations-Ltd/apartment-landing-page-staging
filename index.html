Two things to plan for before work starts

A full refurbishment in a flat comes with two practical questions. Here is how each one works.

You'll need somewhere else to stay

A full refurbishment strips the flat back to a shell. The kitchen and bathroom go on the first day, and a new layout means every room is opened up at once, so there is no way to live there while the work is done.

The upside: with the flat empty, every room is worked on in the right order at the same time, rather than one room at a time around your furniture.

Your estimator will talk you through timings at the free site visit, so you can plan where to stay.

Your building may need to approve the work

Most London flats are leasehold, and many leases need consent from the freeholder or managing agent before major work such as moving walls. Some buildings also have rules on working hours, deliveries and use of the lift.

Getting these approvals is your responsibility. If you'd rather not deal with it, we can appoint an agent to handle it for you, at an additional cost.

Check your lease early, ideally before you settle on the layout.

Questions people ask before booking

Can't see yours? Call 020 3621 8527, Monday to Saturday, 8AM to 7PM.

Can I live in the flat during the refurbishment?

No. A full refurbishment strips the flat back to a shell: the kitchen and bathroom are taken out on the first day, and when the layout is changing, every room is opened up at once. You will need to stay somewhere else until handover. Your estimator will talk you through timings at the free site visit so you can plan ahead.

How long does a full flat refurbishment take?

It depends on the size of the flat, the layout changes and the finishes you choose, so we don't quote a standard figure. Before work starts we agree your start date with you, and your project coordinator keeps you updated on progress through a WhatsApp group. Your estimator will talk you through timings at the free site visit.

Do I need permission from my freeholder or managing agent?

Often, yes. Many leases need the freeholder's or managing agent's consent before major work such as moving walls, and some buildings set rules on working hours, deliveries and use of the lift. Arranging these approvals is your responsibility, so check your lease early. If you'd rather not deal with it, we can appoint an agent to handle it for you, at an additional cost.

Can you change the layout of my flat?

Yes. Most of our full refurbishments include layout changes, from opening the kitchen onto the living room to adding a bedroom or an en suite. That's one reason the whole flat is stripped out at once. Your estimator will look at what is possible at the free site visit, and any approval your building needs comes first.

How much does a full flat refurbishment cost in London?

Every flat is different, so we don't publish set prices. The cost depends on the size of the flat, any layout changes, the kitchen, bathrooms and finishes you choose, and the electrical and plumbing work involved. After one of our estimators has visited, you receive a clear, itemised proposal, so you know exactly what you are paying for before committing to anything.

Do you use subcontractors or your own team?

Both. Strip-out, plastering, carpentry, flooring, kitchen and bathroom fitting and decorating are all done by our own team, so the same people work on your flat from strip-out to handover. Specialist trades are different: electrical work is carried out by NICEIC certified electricians and gas work by Gas Safe registered engineers, partners we work with regularly. We coordinate them, so you deal with one point of contact throughout.

Do you charge a fee for quoting, or use a matching service?

No. The site visit and the written proposal are free, and there is no obligation to proceed. If you contact us directly there is also no agency or matching-platform commission in your quote. Those platforms are free for you to use because they charge the builder a percentage of every job won, and that cost has to be recovered somewhere. Our direct quotes are typically 10–15% lower than the same project priced through a matching service, because the commission is not there to pass on.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. All workmanship is covered by a 12-month guarantee against faulty labour as standard on every project. If anything falls short of your expectations within the first year, we will return and put it right at no additional cost to you. Materials are covered by their manufacturers' warranties, and the work is backed by our £5M Public Liability Insurance.
